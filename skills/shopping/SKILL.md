---
name: shopping
description: The shopping list, kept as an Obsidian note. Add items, remove or tick them off, read the list, clear it.
enabled: true
trusted: true
triggers: [shopping list, grocery list, groceries]
---
The shopping list lives in the Obsidian note "Shopping list". Items are checkboxes; ticked items are bought.
Use shopping_add for "add milk to my shopping list" (several items at once are fine), shopping_remove to
take something off, shopping_tick when something is bought, shopping_list to read it, shopping_clear_done
to remove ticked items, and shopping_clear_all only when the user asks to empty the whole list.
